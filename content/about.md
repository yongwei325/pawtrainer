## Who we are

PawTrainer is a small team of certified, force-free trainers. We started this site because most new dog parents get overwhelmed by conflicting advice — and a lot of that advice is outdated or based on debunked "dominance" myths.

Every guide we publish is:

- **Science-based** — grounded in how dogs actually learn, not old wives' tales.
- **Force-free** — built on rewards and redirection, never fear or pain.
- **Tested at home** — tried with real puppies and real families before it goes live.

## Our promise

We never recommend choke chains, prong collars, shock collars, or punishment-based methods. The kindest approach is also the one that builds the most reliable behavior — and the strongest bond between you and your dog.

## How we make money

This site is reader-supported. Some product links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission (at no extra cost to you) if you buy through them. This never influences what we recommend — we only ever suggest gear we'd use with our own dogs.
